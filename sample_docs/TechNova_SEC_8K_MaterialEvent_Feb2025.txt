ITEM 1.01 — ENTRY INTO A MATERIAL DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------

On February 18, 2025, TechNova Corporation entered into a definitive
Agreement and Plan of Merger (the "Merger Agreement") to acquire
SecureLayer AI, Inc. ("SecureLayer"), a privately held cybersecurity
artificial intelligence company headquartered in San Francisco, CA.

ACQUISITION TERMS:
  Total Consideration:    $1.85 billion
  Structure:
    - Cash:               $1.20 billion (65% of total)
    - TNOV Stock:         $0.65 billion (35% of total; ~2.96M shares at $219.80)
  Payment at closing:     $1.85 billion
  Contingent earnout:     Up to $150 million based on revenue milestones
                          (FY2026 revenue target: $280M)

SECUERLAYER AI — COMPANY OVERVIEW:
  Founded:          2019
  Headquarters:     San Francisco, CA
  Employees:        620
  CEO:              Dr. Priya Nair
  Products:         AI-powered threat detection, zero-trust architecture
                    tooling, autonomous incident response platform
  Annual Revenue:   $184 million (FY2024, audited)
  Revenue Growth:   +68% YoY (FY2024 vs FY2023)
  Gross Margin:     81%
  EBITDA:           ($22 million) — investing phase, negative EBITDA expected
                    to turn positive in FY2026

STRATEGIC RATIONALE:
  1. Extends TechNova AI Suite with native cybersecurity capabilities
  2. Adds 340+ enterprise customers, including 18 Fortune 100 companies
  3. Eliminates a competitive gap versus Peer A's recent cybersecurity push
  4. SecureLayer's threat detection IP (47 patents) is highly complementary
  5. Expected to be EPS accretive by Q4 2026 (post-integration)

FINANCIAL IMPACT:
  Purchase Price / LTM Revenue Multiple:  10.1x ($1.85B / $184M)
  Purchase Price / LTM Gross Profit:      12.2x
  EV / Forward Revenue (2025E, $240M est): 7.7x

  FY2026 revenue contribution expected: $270-285M
  Integration costs (one-time):         $45-60M over 18 months
  EPS dilution (FY2025):               ($0.18) per diluted share
  EPS accretion (FY2026):              $0.22 - $0.28 per diluted share

FINANCING:
  The cash portion ($1.20B) will be funded from existing cash reserves.
  TechNova's pro forma cash position post-close: ~$7.2B.
  No new debt financing is required.

CLOSING CONDITIONS:
  - Regulatory approvals (HSR Act filing submitted Feb 18, 2025)
  - SecureLayer shareholder approval (expected: majority already secured
    via signed support agreements from holders of 78% of shares)
  - Standard representations, warranties, and covenants
  - Expected closing: Q2 2025 (subject to regulatory timing)
