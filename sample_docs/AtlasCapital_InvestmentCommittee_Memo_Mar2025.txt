AGENDA ITEM 2 — GFBG RISK ASSESSMENT
=======================================

BACKGROUND:
GreenFin Banking (GFBG) disclosed an active AML (Anti-Money Laundering)
inquiry by DOJ/FinCEN in its FY2024 10-K filing (Item 3, Legal Proceedings).
This inquiry was NOT disclosed in prior quarterly filings, suggesting it
materialized or escalated in Q4 2024.

RISK ASSESSMENT:
  Best Case:   No charges filed. Regulatory slap on wrist (small fine <$50M).
               Stock impact: -3% to -5% (one-time).

  Base Case:   Deferred Prosecution Agreement (DPA). Fine of $100-300M.
               Stock impact: -10% to -15% initially, recovery within 6 months.

  Worst Case:  Criminal indictment / large fine ($500M+) / management changes.
               Stock impact: -25% to -40%. Bank charter risk is remote but
               cannot be zero.

CURRENT POSITION:
  AUM%:        1.35%  ($840M market value)
  Unrealized gain: $264M
  If worst case, mark-to-market loss: ~$210-336M on this position.

LEGAL TEAM UPDATE (received March 3, 2025):
  Our outside counsel (retained to monitor public filings) notes:
  - DOJ has not filed charges or issued formal civil investigative demands
    as of March 1, 2025.
  - FinCEN is seeking records, not yet issuing consent orders.
  - Timeline: typically 12-24 months from document request to resolution.
  - Comparable cases: JPMorgan (2014) settled AML matter for $2.6B but
    was too big to fail; GFBG at $60B in assets is more vulnerable to
    regulatory action but less likely to face charter risk.

CRO RECOMMENDATION:
  Reduce GFBG from 1.35% to 0.90% AUM immediately as risk mitigation.
  This would realize ~$280M in gains and reduce downside exposure.
  Keep remaining 0.90% as the thesis (rate-cut beneficiary) is intact.

  priced in (GFBG underperformed sector by 8% since 10-K publication).

  forced selling, allowing upside participation if inquiry resolves.

PROPOSED RESOLUTION:

  If triggered, reduce to 0.50% AUM. Revisit in 90 days.

VOTE REQUIRED: Yes (4/7 majority)
