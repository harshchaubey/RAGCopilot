RISKS TO OUR THESIS
--------------------
DOWNSIDE RISKS:
1. AI spending pause: If enterprise buyers delay AI investments amid macro
   uncertainty, software growth could decelerate below 25% in H2 2025.
   (Bear case: $185)

2. Hyperscaler competition: AWS, Azure, and GCP are all building competing
   AI workflow tools. TNOV's moat relies on proprietary data and integrations.

3. Regulatory headwinds: EU AI Act compliance costs could run $50-100M in 2025.

4. Key person risk: CEO Sarah Chen's departure would be a significant negative
   catalyst. Her 4-year RSU cliff vesting (January 2026) reduces near-term risk.

UPSIDE RISKS:
1. Transformative M&A: $8.4B cash + $2.3B buyback flexibility could fund a
   strategic acquisition in cybersecurity or vertical AI. Bull case: $295.

2. Pricing power: TNOV has not raised AI Software pricing since October 2023.
   A 10% price increase would add ~$140M to revenue.

3. International expansion: APAC revenue is only 9% of total. European
   expansion initiative announced for Q3 2025 is not in our base case.
