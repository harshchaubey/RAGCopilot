INVESTMENT POLICY STATEMENT
ATLAS CAPITAL MANAGEMENT — STANDARD EQUITY MANDATE

SECTION 1 — PURPOSE AND SCOPE
--------------------------------
This Investment Policy Statement (IPS) establishes the framework,
objectives, constraints, and guidelines for managing equity portfolios
under Atlas Capital Management's Standard Equity Mandate.

It is designed to:
  1. Clearly communicate the investment philosophy and strategy
  2. Define measurable performance expectations
  3. Set boundaries on investment risk and permissible securities
  4. Establish communication and reporting standards


SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT PHILOSOPHY
------------------------------------
Atlas Capital Management subscribes to a fundamental, bottom-up investment
philosophy with the following core tenets:

2.1 Long-Term Orientation
  We invest with a 3-5 year investment horizon. Short-term price fluctuations
  are viewed as opportunities, not risks. We believe long-term value is
  created by owning high-quality businesses at reasonable prices.

2.2 Quality Focus
  We define "quality" along three dimensions:
    a) Business Quality:    Durable competitive advantages, pricing power,
                            low capital intensity, large total addressable market.
    b) Financial Quality:   Strong free cash flow generation, conservative
                            leverage, high returns on invested capital (ROIC).
    c) Management Quality:  Shareholder-aligned management, transparent
                            communication, prudent capital allocation history.

2.3 Valuation Discipline
  We will not pay any price for a great business. We seek a margin of safety —
  owning excellent businesses at prices that imply reasonable returns even
  in conservative scenarios. Our IRR (Internal Rate of Return) hurdle for
  new positions: minimum 15% annualized over 5 years.

2.4 Concentrated-But-Diversified
  Our typical portfolio holds 25-40 positions. This provides meaningful
  diversification while maintaining enough concentration to drive alpha.
  We believe that owning your 40th-best idea dilutes returns.

2.5 ESG Integration
  Environmental, Social, and Governance factors are integrated into our
  analysis. We do not use ESG as a screening tool to exclude sectors,
  but we do believe companies with poor governance or significant ESG
  risks carry higher cost-of-capital and lower long-term returns.

  EXCLUSION LIST (by IC policy, November 2024):
    - Large-cap social media companies (governance concerns)
    - Manufacturers of controversial weapons
    - Companies with active SEC fraud investigations


SECTION 3 — RETURN AND RISK OBJECTIVES
-----------------------------------------
3.1 Return Objective
  Absolute target:   CPI + 7% annualized over rolling 5-year periods.
  Relative target:   Outperform S&P 500 by 150-250bps annually net of fees.

3.2 Risk Objectives
  Portfolio Beta:            0.85 - 1.05 (vs S&P 500)
  Tracking Error (max):      8.0% annualized
  Maximum Drawdown (soft):   -20% from peak (triggers IC review)
  VaR (99%, 10-day):         Not to exceed 2.5% of portfolio value
  Single Position Max:       2.0% of AUM (hard limit)
  Single Sector Max:         30% of AUM (hard limit)


SECTION 4 — ASSET ALLOCATION AND CONSTRAINTS
-----------------------------------------------
4.1 Permissible Securities
  PERMITTED:
    - U.S.-listed common stocks (NYSE, NASDAQ, NYSE American)
    - ADRs (American Depositary Receipts) of foreign companies
    - ETFs for tactical hedging (max 10% of portfolio)
    - Investment-grade bonds (up to 10% of portfolio for income mandates)
    - Cash and money market instruments (no limit)

  NOT PERMITTED:
    - Derivatives (options, futures) — except for approved hedging programs
    - Commodities (direct ownership)
    - Private placements and illiquid securities (without specific IC approval)
    - Short selling (long-only mandate)
    - Leverage / margin trading

4.2 Liquidity Requirements
  - Minimum 80% of portfolio must be liquidatable within 5 trading days
    without moving the market by more than 1%.
  - No single position to exceed 5% of the underlying company's
    average daily trading volume (90-day average).

4.3 Geographic Constraints
  - U.S. equities:          Minimum 60% of portfolio
  - International (developed): Maximum 30% of portfolio
  - Emerging Markets:       Maximum 10% of portfolio


SECTION 5 — TRADING AND EXECUTION POLICY
-------------------------------------------
5.1 Best Execution
  Atlas Capital is committed to achieving best execution for all client
  trades. Factors considered: price, speed, likelihood of execution,
  total transaction cost, and market impact.

5.2 Trade Allocation
  When multiple client accounts are eligible for the same trade, shares
  are allocated pro-rata based on account size. No client receives
  preferential treatment.

5.3 Soft Dollars
  Atlas Capital uses soft-dollar arrangements to pay for research and
  execution services. Full disclosure of soft-dollar practices is provided
  in our ADV Part 2 document.

5.4 Prohibited Practices
  - Front-running client trades with personal accounts
  - Churning (excessive trading to generate commissions)
  - Window dressing (buying winners before quarter-end for optics)
  - Late trading or market timing


SECTION 6 — PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT & REPORTING
--------------------------------------------------
6.1 Benchmark
  Primary:    S&P 500 Total Return Index
  Secondary:  MSCI World Total Return Index (for international mandates)

6.2 Reporting Schedule
  Monthly:    Performance summary, top 10 holdings, attribution (2 pages)
  Quarterly:  Full portfolio review, market commentary, outlook (10-15 pages)
  Annual:     Comprehensive review, IPS review, fee schedule review

6.3 Performance Calculation
  All performance figures are calculated net of management fees,
  trading commissions, and fund expenses. Gross and net figures
  are reported separately. We comply with GIPS (Global Investment
  Performance Standards) for all performance reporting.

6.4 Fee Schedule (Standard Equity Mandate)
  AUM < $10M:          1.00% annually
  AUM $10M - $50M:     0.75% annually
  AUM $50M - $100M:    0.60% annually
  AUM > $100M:         0.50% annually (negotiable)

  Performance Fee:     10% of annual return above S&P 500 + 200bps hurdle
                       (high-water mark applies)

  Fees billed:         Quarterly in arrears


SECTION 7 — CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
------------------------------------
Atlas Capital discloses the following potential conflicts:

1. Proprietary Trading:
   Atlas Capital may trade for its own account in securities also held
   in client portfolios. Client interests are always prioritized.

2. Research Use:
   Investment ideas may originate from the same research used for
   our proprietary accounts. All clients receive equal access to research.

3. Personal Trading:
   Employee personal trading is governed by a strict Code of Ethics.
   All personal trades require pre-clearance. See Chapter 4 of the
   Employee Handbook (Insider Trading Rules).

4. Board Seats:
   Atlas Capital does not accept corporate board seats to avoid access
   to material non-public information.

5. Referral Arrangements:
   No referral fees are paid to third parties for client introductions.
   All fee arrangements are disclosed in the client agreement.


SECTION 8 — REVIEW AND AMENDMENT
-----------------------------------

Amendments require written consent from both Atlas Capital and the client.
